# Job sites to add to Iris (researched Nov 8, 2026)

Iris uses now: Indeed, Handshake, company career pages, and the n8n digest (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Ashby, Simplify). LinkedIn is for connections only.

## Best to add (in this order)

| Site | What it is | Why it fits a CS new grad on F-1 | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Work at a Startup (workatastartup.com, YC) | YC startups' own job board | Every job has a Visa field ("Will sponsor" / "US citizen/visa only" / "not required") and an experience field ("new grads ok") | Needs an account; Iris has only reached 65 YC jobs through links so far (6 applied) |
| Hiring Cafe (hiring.cafe) | Free search engine that pulls jobs straight from company career pages | Sends you to the company's own page, which is exactly where Iris applies best; Reddit likes it (83k-member subreddit), good filters | Some old or fake listings, dates don't always match |
| Wellfound (wellfound.com, was AngelList) | Biggest startup job site, salary and equity shown | Lots of small companies that hire new grads; free | Applications happen on Wellfound itself with its own profile |
| Jobright (jobright.ai) | AI job matching | Tags postings "H1B Sponsor Likely" / "H1B Sponsorship", useful for finding sponsor-friendly new-grad jobs | Use it only to find jobs: reviews say its resume AI adds skills you don't have |
| Built In (builtin.com) | Tech jobs by city, entry-level category with salaries | Good for Houston, Austin, Dallas tech roles | Some old postings |

## Maybe

- **ZipRecruiter:** huge, but repeats a lot of Indeed and has many staffing agencies. "1-Click Apply" sends its profile resume, not the tailored one.
- **Welcome to the Jungle (Otta merged into it):** curated tech jobs with company profiles, free.
- **Dice:** oldest tech-only board, but heavy on contract and staffing jobs.
- **WayUp, RippleMatch:** early-career sites; mixed reviews.
- **GitHub new-grad lists:** SimplifyJobs/New-Grad-Positions (n8n's Simplify monitor may already cover it), speedyapply/2026-SWE-College-Jobs (updated daily).

## Skip

- **Wonderin:** an AI resume and job-matching tool ($15/month Pro), not a job site with its own jobs.
- **Google for Jobs:** only collects postings from Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter and company pages.
- **Glassdoor:** mostly for company research.
- **Paid sponsor lists (F1Jobs $349–499/month) and auto-apply tools (SpeedyApply $19/month):** Iris already does this work.

## F-1 narrowing (researched Nov 15, 2026; now in Iris's task)

Only about 2.6% of full-time US postings offered sponsorship in May 2026 (Handshake data via CNBC, cited by f1jobs.io), so Iris now spends its search time where employers already hire F-1 OPT students.

| Source | What Iris does there | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Handshake "Accepts OPT/CPT" filter | Jobs → All filters → Work authorization, tick only Accepts OPT/CPT; then the same search without it | Ticking both boxes hides most jobs; many employers leave it blank |
| Indeed with sponsorship words | "software engineer H1B sponsorship", "OPT software developer", "STEM OPT data engineer", Houston first | Staffing firms posting W2 OPT roles show up; corp-to-corp stays out |
| Texas H-1B sponsors (myvisajobs FY2025 LCA counts) | One employer's own careers site per round | Houston: Baylor College of Medicine 254, Rice 108, JPMorgan Chase 97, Houston Methodist Research Institute 94, SLB 84, NOV 37, Honeywell 35. Texas: Charles Schwab 439, Fidelity 611, AT&T 340, American Airlines 325, Visa 313, Goldman Sachs 389, Citi 543, U.S. Bank 264, FedEx 231, Tesla 530 |
| Private universities | Rice, Baylor College of Medicine, SMU, TCU, Baylor University | Cap-exempt: H-1B without the lottery |
| zapply new grad list (GitHub) | Only rows marked "✅ Sponsor" | The only public list found with a visa column |
| IT services firms | Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, HCLTech, Wipro, LTIMindtree, Mphasis, Tech Mahindra, Capgemini, Compunnel, Accenture, EY | Largest Texas H-1B filers; hire OPT graduates on W2 |

**Not added:**
- **Texas public universities and agencies:** UH, UT Austin, UT Southwestern, MD Anderson, UTHealth and Texas A&M are under Gov. Abbott's freeze on new H-1B petitions (Jan 27, 2026 to May 31, 2027). Iris still applies there when a posting fits, but doesn't search their sites.
- **Sites that need a login or block automated readers:** hiring.cafe (403), Work at a Startup and Jobright (no listings without logging in).
- **Built In:** has no visa filter.
- **Lists without a visa column:** speedyapply and jobright's GitHub lists.
