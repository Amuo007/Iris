Welcome to Iris

Iris is a browser you can use, and an assistant you can delegate to. Everything you expect from a browser is here; when a task is tedious, describe it and Iris drives the page for you.

Ask, don't click

Press ⌘ ⇧ Space to open the Iris panel, describe the outcome you want, then watch it work step by step.

Everything is shown

Every step lists what Iris saw, what it decided and what it did, with the screenshot it acted on. Pause, stop, or just take over at any moment.

Your model, your machine

Point Iris at any OpenAI-compatible vision model in Settings → Iris AI. Nothing about the model leaves your network unless you send it there.

How Iris looks and acts

Iris only ever sees screenshots of the page and sends real mouse and keyboard events, the same as you. It never reads a page's markup or runs code inside the sites you visit, so a site can only show Iris the same thing it shows you. Purchases, payments and form submissions are refused unless you turn them on in settings.

Iris is built on the excellent Min browser (Apache-2.0), which remains the foundation for tabs, history, bookmarks, search and privacy protection.
